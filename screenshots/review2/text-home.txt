GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Text Dawn at (301) 461-3147.

Where is Style by Dawn?

Inside Shepherd's Old Field Market at 22725 Duke Street, Leonardtown, MD 20650, about a block off the square.

What are your hours?

Tuesday to Saturday 10 AM to 7 PM, Sunday 11 AM to 5 PM. Closed Monday.

What sizes do you carry?

Sizes change with every delivery. Text Dawn at (301) 461-3147 with the piece you like and she will tell you what is in.

Can I shop online?

Not yet. Everything is in the shop, and you can text Dawn to ask about a piece first.

What does it cost?

Dawn keeps her prices affordable. Ask about any piece by text.

Do you have a second shop?

Yes, Style House on Park Avenue, inside The Inn at Leonardtown.
