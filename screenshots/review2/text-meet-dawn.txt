THE OWNER

Meet Dawn, owner of Style by Dawn in Leonardtown

Dawn runs Style by Dawn, a women's boutique inside Shepherd's Old Field Market on Duke Street in Leonardtown, Maryland.

The door is open on Duke Street.
A Leonardtown story.

Dawn grew up in St. Mary's County. Dawn has run boutiques at Shepherd's Old Field Market since it opened in 2016. Every week she dresses Stiffany, the shop mannequin, in the newest arrivals, and she will help you put the whole outfit together.

Her aim fits on one line:

“Women's fashion at affordable prices to help you feel fabulous every day!”
